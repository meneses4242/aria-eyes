## 🔧 Setting Up Your First Export

### Connect Your LinkedIn Account

After installation, the app will ask you to connect to LinkedIn. It opens your own Chrome browser, so you simply:

1. Log into LinkedIn normally
2. Navigate to your job posting that has applicants
3. The application will automatically detect the applicant list

### Start an Export

1. Click the **"Start Export"** button in the app dashboard
2. The app will slowly go through each applicant - just like a human would
3. You'll see progress in the dashboard showing how many applicants have been processed
4. When finished, you'll get a complete list with links to each profile and resume

## ⏱️ Patience is Built-In

This application works at a human-like pace. It's designed this way to keep your LinkedIn account safe. Here's what that means:

- **It waits** between each action - just like reading and clicking
- **It scrolls** through pages naturally
- **It pauses** at random intervals to look normal
- **It won't** process hundreds of applicants in seconds

A typical export of 50 applicants might take about 10-15 minutes. Larger lists take longer, but everything runs automatically while you work on something else.
